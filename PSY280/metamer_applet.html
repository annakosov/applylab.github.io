Metamer Spectrum Drawer

Paint a photon spectrum by wavelength and see how the long (L), medium (M), and short (S) wavelength cones would respond. The cone responses signal the colour shown under "predicted colour". Many different spectra can excite the L, M, and S cones identically and look the same. These are called metamers. Use the plot on the right to draw a different spectrum that produces the same colour appearance.
